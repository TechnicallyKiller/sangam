# Cover letter — author review draft

Dear Editor,

Please consider “Sangam: A Blockchain-Coordinated Prototype for Auditable Federated Learning
in Healthcare” for Informatica's Normal (N) review track.

The manuscript presents an inspectable research prototype combining reputation-weighted
federated learning, privacy accounting, and Solidity-based model provenance. It evaluates
utility, a privacy–utility sweep, and simple poisoning/free-riding attacks over ten random
seeds on the public Breast Cancer Wisconsin benchmark. A separate integrated implementation
executes real local-Ethereum transactions and verifies stored model bytes against commitments.

The contribution is the reproducible integration and evaluation, rather than a new privacy
proof or a claim of clinical validation. The manuscript states its limitations, including
validator visibility, simulated masking, centralized simulation preprocessing, and the lack
of a public-testnet deployment. Code, configuration, per-seed outputs, and model/receipt
provenance accompany the work.
