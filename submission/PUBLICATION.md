# Publication without paying

## Recommended journal route: Informatica, Normal track

The journal's current submission guidance lists the **Normal (N)** track as having **no fee**,
with a target first decision of **6–12 months**. Its optional ultra-fast track costs money and
is outside this project's ₹0 budget. It uses peer review; neither acceptance nor publication
before 20 November can be promised.

Official instructions: https://www.informatica.si/index.php/informatica/about/submissions

Why this choice: computing scope, a stated free track, editable Word/LaTeX templates, and an
explicit policy allowing disclosed AI assistance. This manuscript includes that disclosure.
A journal with a free but lengthy review process is different from a guaranteed fast publication.

## Small items the authors must complete before external submission

1. Supply the corresponding author's preferred email and confirm the actual contribution roles.
2. Have the guide/team review the manuscript, references, results, and AI-assistance declaration.
3. Confirm conflicts, originality, and exclusive submission; these statements must be truthful.
4. Review the supplied journal-styled manuscript against the latest instructions, then upload it with the figures and cover letter.
5. Select **N**, never the paid UF option. Keep the actual submission acknowledgment as evidence.

The college notice requires publication but does not specify whether submission proof or a
preprint counts. Ask the mentor to confirm what can be accepted for the immediate defence.

## If the mentor accepts a preprint

IEEE describes TechRxiv as a free preprint server for engineering and computer science:
https://innovate.ieee.org/open-science-solutions/

A preprint is public dissemination before formal peer review. It must not be described as a
peer-reviewed journal publication. Posting is subject to server availability, screening, and
all authors' agreement. No preprint has been posted or submission receipt fabricated.

Preprints.org is also free, but its instructions normally exclude coursework; the project
would need to qualify as a research article. It is not treated as a guaranteed shortcut:
https://www.preprints.org/instructions-for-authors

Sources checked 16 November 2026. No paid fast-track or pay-to-accept outlet is part of this plan.
